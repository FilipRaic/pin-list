To-do lists on your desktop and in your pocket

PinList keeps your lists as small widgets on the Windows and macOS desktop, and as cards on your Android phone. Categories, due dates, reminders and an archive of everything you finished. No account needed.

What it does

  • Lists that stay on the desktop

    Every list is a borderless widget. On Windows it survives Show desktop and stays out of the taskbar. On macOS it is on every Space.

  • The same lists on your phone

    The Android app shows a card for every list with its color, item count and overdue items. Tap one to open it full screen.

  • Categories and drag and drop

    Group items in colored categories and drag items, categories and lists into the order you want.

  • Due dates and reminders

    Overdue items turn red, and reminders arrive as system notifications, on the phone also when the app is closed.

  • Archive and undo

    Completed items go to a searchable archive. Ctrl+Z on the desktop and the Undo bar on the phone bring back almost any change.

  • Optional Google Drive sync

    Choose which lists sync between your PCs, Macs and phone. Changes are merged item by item, also after days offline.

Your data stays yours

Everything you put into PinList is saved on your device, in a plain JSON file you can export at any time. There is no PinList server, and nothing leaves your device unless you turn on sync.

With sync, your lists go to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. PinList can't see any of your other Drive files, and the developer never receives your data.

Read the privacy policy

In short
  • Your lists stay on your devices
  • Sync is optional and off until you turn it on
  • Synced lists go only to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive
  • The developer never receives, sees or stores your data
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking
