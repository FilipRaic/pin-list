Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026

PinList is a to-do list app for Windows, macOS and Android, made by one developer as an open-source project (source code). This page explains what happens with your data.

In short: your lists stay on your devices. If you turn on the optional Google Drive sync, they are also stored in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, so your devices can share them. If you allow it, PinList also asks GitHub about once a day whether there is a newer version. The developer never receives, sees or stores any of your data, and there is no PinList server. The only exception is feedback you choose to send.

Without sync (the default)

  • Everything you put into PinList (lists, items, categories, due dates, reminders, the archive and settings) is saved only on your device, in PinList's data folder:
    • Windows: %APPDATA%\PinList\
    • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/PinList/
    • Android: the app's private storage
  • PinList doesn't need an account. It doesn't connect to the internet, except for the update check if you turn it on.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting. Errors are written only to a local file (log.txt) in the data folder.
  • Export creates a file wherever you choose to save it. What happens with that file is up to you.

Update check (optional, you are asked first)

The first time you start a PinList version that has it, PinList asks whether it may look for new versions. Until you say yes, it doesn't. If you say yes:

  • About once a day, PinList asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the number and the release notes of PinList's latest release, the same information anyone sees on the releases page.
  • The request contains nothing about you, your lists or your settings, and no id of your device. Like any visit to a website, GitHub sees your IP address and that the request comes from PinList. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • When there is a newer version, PinList tells you once. Get it downloads it in your browser (or opens the download section of this website). PinList never installs anything by itself.
  • Turn it off or on at any time with Check for updates in Settings, on the desktop and on Android.

Feedback (only what you send)

Send feedback in Settings opens the feedback page of this website in your browser. PinList itself sends nothing. The page's address contains the PinList version and your system with its version (for example Windows 11 24H2 (build 26100.2894), macOS 15.1, or on Android also the phone's model: Android 15 (Google Pixel 9)), which fill in the Device field. You can change or clear it before sending.

  • By email: the form is relayed to the developer's inbox by FormSubmit, because this website has no server. It sends what you wrote, the kind of feedback, the Device field and your email address if you give one (only needed for a reply). FormSubmit sees your IP address, like any website. See FormSubmit's privacy policy.
  • As a GitHub issue: the form opens GitHub with the text filled in, and nothing is sent until you post it there. Issues are public. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Feedback emails are used only to read and answer your feedback. They are never shared or sold, and you can ask for yours to be deleted by replying to the answer, or through the feedback page.

With Google Drive sync (optional, off by default)

Sync is off until you sign in with Google and turn it on for a list. Lists you don't turn it on for never leave your device.

Where your data goes. Synced lists are stored only in the app data folder of your own Google Drive (Google's appDataFolder): a hidden folder that only PinList can use and that doesn't appear among your Drive files. Your devices signed in with the same Google account read and write it directly. Nobody else's devices and no PinList server are involved.

What is stored there for each synced list: its title, color, categories, items (text, creation time, due date, reminder), its completed items, and technical information PinList needs to merge changes correctly (ids, change times and counters, and the name of each of your devices, such as your computer's name or your phone's model, so messages can say where a change came from).

What PinList can access in your Google account. PinList asks only for:

Permission Why
Its own app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata) To store and read your synced lists. PinList can't see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
Your email address and Google account id (openid, userinfo.email) To show "Signed in as …" and to tell your accounts apart. They're stored only on your device.

Your sign-in stays on your device: - Windows: encrypted for your Windows user account (Windows DPAPI), in google-token.dat in the data folder - macOS: in your login Keychain - Android: kept by Google Play services, PinList doesn't store a sign-in token itself

On Android, PinList also syncs in the background (about every 15 minutes, only while you're signed in and at least one list is synced), using Android's standard background job system.

Signing out removes your sign-in from that device only. Your other devices stay signed in. Sign out and delete cloud data also tells Google to revoke PinList's access, which signs out every device. Your lists stay on your devices either way.

Deleting your data

  • On a device: delete lists in PinList, or uninstall PinList and delete its data folder (on Android, uninstalling removes the data).
  • In Google Drive: in PinList choose Sign out and delete cloud data, or in Google Drive go to Settings → Manage apps → PinList → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove PinList's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PinList's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to provide sync to you, is never transferred to anyone else, is never used for advertising, and is never read by a person.

Children

PinList isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything about anyone. It sends your data nowhere except your own Google Drive, and the feedback you choose to send.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date, and the change is noted in the changelog.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: use the feedback page, or open an issue at https://github.com/FilipRaic/pin-list/issues.
